FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a vehicle?

Use the Customer Portal, call our dispatch line, or send us a message. We confirm the vehicle, price and schedule within hours.

What materials can you carry?

Construction materials, containers, machinery, agricultural produce, refrigerated goods and general cargo up to 45 tons per trip.

How is the price calculated?

Based on distance, weight and load type. Every completed trip is recorded in our system with exact km, weight and fuel for full transparency.

Can I track my shipment?

Yes. Enter your booking reference on the Customer Portal to see live status: loading, in transit or delivered.
